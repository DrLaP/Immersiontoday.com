The Preparation Framework

The Four C's of Effective Testimony

Every dimension of witness performance is organized within this research-grounded framework — developed by Justin LaPilusa, Psy.D., Q.M.E.

C
Competence

Factual preparation, prior statement review, and thorough knowledge of the account. The domain most witnesses address — and the only one traditional preparation covers.

C
Composure

Management of the stress response. Breath work, mindfulness, ACT, and the physical preparation that keeps the nervous system in the range where it serves rather than undermines performance.

C
Character

Credibility and likeability — the two dimensions on which the jury evaluates every witness. Voice, body language, eye contact, and the behavioral signals that communicate trustworthiness.

C
Combatant

Opposing counsel's cross-examination strategies — the twenty tactics, the logical fallacies, the emotional provocations, and the prepared witness's responses to each.

The Book

The truth is not self-presenting. It requires a vehicle. This manual prepares the vehicle.

Every year, honest witnesses lose credibility on the stand — not because their accounts are inaccurate, but because the physiological stress response produces behaviors that juries read as deception. Prepared is the first comprehensive, science-based manual to address the physical, psychological, and communicative dimensions of testimony that determine whether truthful accounts are received as credible ones.

  • The science of why honest witnesses look guilty — and what closes the gap
  • Kahneman's dual-process theory applied to jury decision-making
  • The biology of fight-flight-freeze-fawn — and how to regulate it
  • Mindfulness, ACT, and cognitive behavioral frameworks for testimony performance
  • The actor's method: Stanislavski, Adler, Hagen, and the Alexander Technique
  • 20 cross-examination strategies opposing counsel will use — and the responses
  • The Stoic philosophy of Marcus Aurelius applied to composure under pressure
  • A complete, sequenced trial-day protocol from the week before to the stand
