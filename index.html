The Preparation Framework

The Four C's of Effective Testimony

Every dimension of witness performance is organized within this research-grounded framework.

C
Competence

Factual preparation, prior statement review, and thorough knowledge of the account. The domain most witnesses already address — and the only one traditional preparation covers.

C
Composure

Management of the stress response. Breath work, mindfulness, ACT, and the physical preparation that keeps the nervous system in the range where it serves rather than undermines performance.

C
Character

Credibility and likeability — the two dimensions on which the jury evaluates every witness. Voice, body language, eye contact, facial expression, and the behavioral signals that communicate trustworthiness.

C
Combatant

Opposing counsel's cross-examination strategies — the twenty tactics, the logical fallacies, the emotional provocations, and the prepared witness's responses to each. Understanding the terrain before you enter it.
